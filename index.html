📋 Scope of Work

⚠️ Hazard Identification & Controls

Hazard Risk Control / Mitigation
Energized electrical equipment HIGH LOTO procedures, hot-gloves, rubber blankets, voltage test before contact
Working in or near traffic HIGH Traffic control plan, cones/signs, hi-vis PPE, spotter assigned
Excavation / trench collapse HIGH Utility locate (811), slope/shore/shield per OSHA, competent-person inspection
Overhead power lines HIGH Maintain min. 10 ft clearance, spotter, equipment barriers
Slips / trips / falls MED Housekeeping, steel-toe boots, wet surface awareness
Hand / pinch points (tools & equipment) MED Cut-resistant gloves, proper tool use, no bypassing guards
Heat / sun exposure MED Hydration, shade breaks, buddy system for heat-illness signs
Confined space (manholes / vaults) HIGH Permit-required CS procedure, gas monitor, attendant, rescue plan
Chemical / chlorine exposure (water ops) MED SDS review, chemical gloves, eye wash station nearby
Other (specify) LOW

✅ Pre-Job Checklist

  • Work order / permit reviewed and understood by all crew members
  • 811 utility locates called & clearance obtained (marks visible on-site)
  • LOTO / energy isolation verified if applicable
  • Traffic control plan in place (cones, signs, flaggers)
  • Confined space entry permit issued if entering vault / manhole
  • Gas / atmosphere monitor calibrated and on-site (O₂, LEL, H₂S, CO)
  • First aid kit and fire extinguisher in crew vehicle
  • Emergency contact numbers posted / known (911, supervisor, dispatch)
  • All crew hold required qualifications / licenses for today's tasks
  • Equipment pre-use inspections completed (truck, excavator, chain saw, etc.)
  • SDS sheets available for any chemicals on-site
  • Weather conditions reviewed — work adjusted if needed
  • Work site access & egress path identified
  • Nearest hospital / urgent care identified:
  • Any crew members have physical limitations or medical conditions to be aware of today? If yes, noted in comments.
